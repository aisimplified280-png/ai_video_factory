# Cinematic Quality Judge

Inspect encoded frames for:
- blur and resampling artifacts
- flicker
- aliasing
- broken SVGs
- clipped elements
- inconsistent scaling
- accidental masks
- compression damage
- extreme contrast
- accidental color regressions
- dead/frozen compositions
- awkward motion or abrupt snapping

Score technical visual quality separately from semantic relevance.

A semantically correct scene with obvious rendering defects fails.
