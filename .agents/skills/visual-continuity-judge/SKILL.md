# Visual Continuity Judge

For each adjacent scene pair, inspect:
- outgoing focal point
- incoming focal point
- camera direction
- scale relationship
- character continuity
- environment progression
- object/state progression
- transition semantics

Reject arbitrary jumps that look like unrelated slides stitched together.

Continuity may intentionally reset for a new story beat, but the reason should be visible and editorially coherent.
