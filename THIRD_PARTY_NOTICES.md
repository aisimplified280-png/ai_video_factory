# Third-Party Skill Inspiration and Attribution

The new skill guides in this repository are independently written adaptations of general directing and animation techniques. They do not vendor upstream executable scripts, renderers, assets, demo films or fonts.

- **Lemo-Opuscar** — LemoLab, MIT License. Repository: https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar. The 10 technique guides link to their corresponding upstream STYLE.md files.
- **Cinetic** — Leonxlnx, MIT License. Repository: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/cinetic. Only concept-first direction, story-device selection, beat timing and sound-picture coordination principles are adapted. Its default visual bans, stack and dependencies are not imported.
- **Pixel2Motion** — Nolan Lai, MIT License. Repository: https://github.com/nolangz/pixel2motion. Only minimal motion-ready vector geometry, animation-principle and final-frame verification ideas are adapted. Its scripts and Python/Playwright setup are not imported.

No upstream source code or third-party assets are copied into this skill pack. Any future direct import of upstream code, scripts, assets or substantial text must include the applicable upstream copyright and license notices and must be reviewed before inclusion.
