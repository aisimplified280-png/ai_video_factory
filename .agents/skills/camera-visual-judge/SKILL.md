# Camera Visual Judge

Inspect full-scene frame sequences.

Verify that camera intent such as:
- push-in
- pull-out
- pan
- tracking
- focus shift
- overhead
- parallax
- zoom through

produces visible camera behavior.

Do not give credit when only object opacity or tiny decorative motion changes.

Record frame ranges and measured displacement of dominant visual structures.
